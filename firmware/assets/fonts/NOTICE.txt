These modified/subsetted assets are named Weather Display UI Subset. They contain
216 printable ASCII, Latin/symbol and Russian/Ukrainian Cyrillic glyphs for the
UI at 12, 14 and 20 pixels. ASCII glyphs come from LVGL's Montserrat Medium
bitmap fonts. All non-ASCII glyphs are rasterised directly from the pinned
Montserrat Light TTF. Unsupported characters use the '?' glyph.
No Font Awesome or weather icon glyphs are included.

Modifications: retain the LVGL ASCII raster shapes, advances, offsets and
kerning, remove unused glyphs, and convert metadata to little-endian binary
records. Rasterise the non-ASCII glyphs using Pillow 9.0.1 and FreeType 2.11.1,
quantise coverage to four bits and store kerning as sorted sparse pairs.
The coverage includes accented Latin letters and symbols, all 66 Russian
uppercase/lowercase letters including Ё/ё, and the eight additional Ukrainian
letters Ґ/ґ, Є/є, І/і and Ї/ї. Text bands include Latin accents and Cyrillic
descenders without clipping.
The renderer aligns both weights to a common baseline and blends coverage into
RGB565. The 20px subset serves headings, 14px serves body/control text and 12px
serves secondary labels. Bitmap assets remain OFL-1.1, separately from the
MIT firmware and conversion script.

Regenerate from the project root:
  python3 tools/generate_fonts.py
Verify reproducibility without rewriting assets:
  python3 tools/generate_fonts.py --check
The converter requires Pillow 9.0.1 built with FreeType 2.11.1 (versions are
checked against sources.json), fetches pinned source files only when absent in
firmware/target/font-sources, and checks their SHA-256 hashes. Pillow is a
development tool; its source and license are at
https://github.com/python-pillow/Pillow/blob/9.0.1/LICENSE.
Device builds and runtime use the checked-in assets and need no font downloads.

The weather clock adds a 42px Montserrat Medium numeric subset, converted from
the same pinned LVGL 9.2.2 sources. It includes digits, colon, hyphen, space and
question mark and retains the same OFL-1.1 terms.

Week and Hours add 19px, 22px and 31px Montserrat Medium subsets rasterized
from Montserrat-Medium.ttf at the same pinned Montserrat commit above (exact URL
and SHA-256 in sources.json). ASCII, localized characters (including all Russian and Ukrainian letters), the degree sign and
arrow use this single weight. The same pinned Pillow/FreeType versions produce
four-bit coverage, advances in sixteenths of a pixel and sparse kerning tables.
Text bands use 23/26/38px heights and 19/21/31px baselines so accents and descenders
remain visible. These assets retain the OFL-1.1 terms and attribution above.
